Ovenlight

The web apps you run on your own computer, on your iPhone behind Face ID. Share one with a friend by link. They install Ovenlight and tap Join.

Got an invite? Install Ovenlight, then open your link again.

Who it's for

You built a small web app for your family, your garden or your book club, maybe with an AI coding tool over a weekend, and it runs on your own computer. Ovenlight puts it on your iPhone without putting it on the internet, and hands it to the people it's for. They need nothing but Ovenlight.

Like real apps, but yours

Your apps, full screen

Each app opens full screen from its own icon in a launcher that works like your Home Screen. No tabs to hunt through, and each app keeps its storage separate from the others.

A private connection, built in

Ovenlight reaches your computers over your own Tailscale network through a connection built into the app. Your iPhone needs no VPN and no Tailscale app.

One unlock

Face ID or your passcode opens all your apps at once. While Ovenlight is locked, no app can use the camera or microphone.

Share one app, not your computer

A friend you invite opens that one app and reaches none of your other devices.

Why not a VPN and a bookmark?

That works for you. For a friend, it means a VPN app and an account. With Ovenlight they get one app with its own icon, nothing to set up, and you can take it back whenever you like. Nothing passes through servers of ours, and Ovenlight has no account, analytics or ads.

Share an app in four steps

  1. You make an app shareable once, on the computer that runs it.

  2. You send an invite from Ovenlight or from that computer: a link or a QR code that works once and expires after 24 hours.

  3. Your friend opens it on their iPhone and taps Join, with no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

  4. The app shows up in their Ovenlight marked with your name, and they can send you feedback from inside it.

You can stop sharing at any time. Their access ends at once, and the app and everything it stored are removed from their iPhone the next time Ovenlight connects.

What you need

An iPhone with iOS 18.1 or later.

To run your own apps, you also need a computer that stays on and awake while anyone uses them, with the Ovenlight connector, and a Tailscale account. The connector runs on a Mac with macOS 13 or later (kept logged in) and on Linux with systemd. Windows comes later.

Ovenlight is free, and so is the connector. Set up your computer.

Your apps are already running. Put them on your iPhone.
